Research

People, work, and intelligent technology.

My research sits at the intersection of industrial-organizational psychology, personnel assessment, and artificial intelligence. I study conversational AI for personality and employment assessment, with a focus on how people interpret and respond to emerging technologies in organizational settings.

I also work on psychological safety, workplace inclusion and telework, and employee experiences in high-reliability settings. Across these topics, I aim to connect rigorous research with practical ways to build fairer, healthier, and more effective organizations.
